## Part D: Fund quality gates and the scoring model

### D.1 Data and frequency

All scored metrics come from public AMFI NAV history (daily NAV per scheme, pulled through mfapi.in). The screen runs fortnightly on the 1st and 15th, on Direct–Growth schemes only. A fund needs **at least 5 years of NAV history** to be scored.

### D.2 Scored metrics

Each metric is ranked as a percentile (0–100) inside its slot, against every other scheme in that slot plus the slot's index fund. The composite score is the weighted average.

| Metric | Formula | Period | Better | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Rolling 3-year return | Median of 3-year CAGRs, measured at each month-end | 25 windows over the last 2 years | Higher | 35% |
| Consistency vs index | Share of those 25 windows in which the fund beat the slot index | Same | Higher | 15% |
| Sortino ratio | Annualised mean monthly return ÷ annualised downside deviation (target 0) | Last 36 months | Higher | 20% |
| Downside capture | Sum of fund returns in months the index fell ÷ sum of index returns in those months × 100 | Last 36 months | Lower | 20% |
| Maximum drawdown | Largest fall from a peak in daily NAV | Last 5 years | Smaller | 10% |

### D.3 Slot index (benchmark and passive option)

| Slot | Index used | Index fund chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Flexi, Contra / value | Nifty 500 | Longest-running plain Nifty 500 index fund |
| Mid | Nifty Midcap 150 | Longest-running plain Midcap 150 index fund |
| Small | Nifty Smallcap 250 | Longest-running plain Smallcap 250 index fund |
| Large & mid | Nifty LargeMidcap 250 | Longest-running plain LargeMidcap 250 index fund |
| US broad | S&P 500 total return, in INR | Longest-running S&P 500 index fund |
| US thematic **[PROPOSED]** | See §I.3. Not screened | None |
| China / EM **[PROPOSED]** | MSCI Golden Dragon, net total return, in INR. Not screened | None |

Factor and smart-beta index funds (momentum, value, quality, equal weight, low volatility) are excluded from the passive option. The best-scoring plain index fund in each slot is ranked alongside the active funds. The international slot benchmarks are used for measurement (Part N). Only US broad is screened.

### D.4 Pass/fail gates (checked before any money moves)

These are not in the score, because AMFI NAV data does not carry them. They are checked by hand at the action step.

| AUM | Small cap ₹1,000–20,000 cr; Mid cap ₹1,000–40,000 cr; Flexi, contra, large & mid ≥ ₹1,000 cr; international FoF ≥ ₹300 cr | AMC factsheet |

**Evidence rule [PROPOSED].** A gate is PASS only when its value comes from an AMC, AMFI or SID document and carries its own date. A figure taken from a fund-ranking or aggregator website may be shown as INDICATIVE but never as PASS, and where sources disagree the latest official disclosure wins (Part M). For an international FoF the all-in expense ratio is the FoF's own ratio plus the underlying fund's ratio when the factsheet states the two separately.

### D.5 Exit trigger from the score

A held fund that sits in the **bottom quartile of its slot at two consecutive quarterly action reviews** goes to exit review (§G.1). A single bad score is a signal, not an action.

## Part E: Entry discipline and the challenger rule

### E.1 What gets screened

Every fortnight, every Direct–Growth scheme in each screened slot's AMFI category, plus the slot index fund. Thematic and frozen slots (US thematic, China / EM) are not screened; they are reviewed by hand at the annual restatement.

### E.2 Who can challenge

A fund not currently held becomes a **candidate** when, in a screen, it is:
1. in the top quartile of its slot, and
2. at least **10 points** above the slot incumbent's composite score.

### E.3 When a candidate qualifies

A candidate becomes a **qualified challenger** when it meets E.2 in **two consecutive fortnightly screens** and passes every §D.4 gate when checked by hand.

A qualified challenger:
- receives all new money and redeployed sale proceeds directed to its slot from that point on, and
- replaces the incumbent outright only under §G.2.

### E.4 Passive challenger

The slot index fund is a candidate like any other. If an index fund qualifies under E.3, the slot moves to passive. Separately, an active incumbent that has beaten its slot index in fewer than 50% of rolling 3-year windows at two consecutive quarterly action reviews loses the slot to the index fund.

### E.5 Lean-book check

A challenger takes a slot; it never adds one. Fund count stays within 8–10.

### E.6 One-line thesis

Before buying a new fund, write one line: what role it plays and what would make you sell it.

## Part F: Deploying money

### F.1 Algorithm (new money and sale proceeds) [PROPOSED]

Every amount is worked out from the latest NAV-based weights (Part M). A fund never receives money merely because its slot is underweight.

1. **Precedence of limits.** Check in this order, and stop at the first that blocks: (a) hard prohibitions (Part L) and hard limits (§B.2); (b) category limits (§B.2); (c) bucket bands; (d) slot shares; (e) fund choice and challenger ranking (§E); (f) market-regime pace.
2. **Regime (pace only).** Nifty 500 above a rising 200-DMA: deploy in up to 2 tranches. Below the 200-DMA: deploy faster, in 3 tranches. The regime never changes what is bought.
3. **Bucket.** Send money to the bucket furthest below its band. If none is below band, to the bucket furthest below its target (largest gap in rupees). A bucket at or above its target gets nothing, even when it is inside its band.
4. **Slot.** Within the bucket, to the slot furthest below its slot share.
5. **Fund.** To the slot's qualified challenger if there is one, otherwise the incumbent. Never to a fund marked consolidate, exit or frozen.
6. **Amount.** The least of: the amount the slot is short of its share; the room left under the 22% ceiling; the room left under the 18% at-cost limit; the room left in the category limit; and the money available. A single-fund slot stops at the 18% cost limit (§B.4).
7. **International.** Only if the AMC's subscription window is open with headroom that day. If it is closed, go to the next most under-target bucket that is eligible under step 3. If none is, record an OPEN decision (D-06) instead of forcing the money into a bucket above its target.
8. **Size.** ≤ ₹1 L or index below 200-DMA: one shot. Above ₹1 L in a normal regime: 2–3 tranches over 4–8 weeks. Re-check §B.2 after each tranche.
9. **Log.** The twelve fields in §M.6.

### F.2 Nothing idle, nothing forced

When a sale is made under §G, the full proceeds go through §F.1 at the same action review. Proceeds that no eligible destination can take are not forced into a bucket above its target. They are recorded as OPEN (D-06) and, until the investor decides, may sit in a Direct–Growth liquid fund for no longer than 30 days.

## Part G: Exit discipline

1. **Thesis or quality break: exit in full, at once.** Mandate drift, AMC governance event, a manager change that removes the reason for owning the fund, or §D.5. Price is irrelevant to this trigger.
2. **Displacement by a qualified challenger: switch at a quarterly action review**, only if all of these hold:
   - the incumbent has been in the bottom half of its slot at two consecutive action reviews;
   - the estimated tax plus exit load on the switch is below 2% of the amount moved; if it is higher, the incumbent is frozen and the challenger receives new money only, until the cost falls (for example after the next ₹1.25 L LTCG exemption resets);
   - no more than one incumbent is replaced per quarter.
3. **Consolidation or cap breach: trim or merge** at the next action review, tax-staggered under §H, and only after the consolidation control in §G.5 has been run.
4. **Mandate change: restructure** at the annual restatement.

Never exit a quality fund only because it fell. Never hold a broken fund only to avoid booking a gain or loss.

### G.5 Consolidation control [PROPOSED]

A consolidation is not PASS because the fund count falls. Before any consolidation the dashboard computes each of these from the latest NAVs and actual units, and shows the NAV date used:

1. current weight of the fund being redeemed and of the fund receiving money;
2. current weight of the bucket;
3. the amount that can be transferred: the least of the 22% ceiling room, the 18% at-cost room and the amount needed to reach the slot target (zero when the bucket is at or above target);
4. the proposed weight of the receiving fund and of the bucket after the transaction, at the 22% ceiling and at the 18% cost limit;
5. the residual proceeds, and where §F.1 sends them;
6. estimated tax, by lot, using the rules in §H.2;
7. estimated exit load, by lot;
8. any transaction restriction (international windows, minimum lots);
9. whether the redeemed fund fails a gate in §D.4;
10. a status from §M.5. The consolidation is executable only when every input is verified and every check passes.

## Part H: Rebalancing and tax

### H.1 Rebalancing

- Band-based. Act when a bucket breaches its band or a fund breaches a §B.2 cap.
- Rebalance with new money and redeployed proceeds first. Selling only to rebalance is the last resort.

### H.2 Tax rules as understood on 4 Nov 2026 [confirm with CA]

- **Domestic equity funds** (flexi, contra, mid, small, large & mid, equity index funds): LTCG at 12.5% above ₹1.25 L a year after 12 months; STCG at 20%. Unaffected by your slab.
- **International FoFs:** from 1 April 2025 these are no longer "specified mutual funds" (that definition now covers funds with more than 65% in debt). For redemptions from FY 2025–26, gains on units held **more than 24 months** are long-term and taxed at **12.5%** without indexation, with no ₹1.25 L exemption. Gains on units held **24 months or less** are short-term and taxed at your **slab rate**. Sources disagree on units bought between 1 April 2023 and 31 March 2025, so confirm the treatment of each lot before selling.
- **New Income-tax Act 2025** took effect on 1 April 2026 and renumbers the sections. Confirm the rates above against it at the annual restatement.

### H.3 What this means for the book

- v1.0 treated the 0% slab as a reason to realise the China gains early. That no longer holds. With a salaried slab, short-term gains on international FoFs are taxed at the slab rate, so **hold each international lot past 24 months before redeeming it** unless §G.1 applies. The China lots bought in March and August 2025 cross 24 months in March and August 2027.
- Use the ₹1.25 L domestic equity LTCG exemption every financial year for consolidations (Bandhan, the losing mid-cap fund).
- The switching-cost test in §G.2 uses these rules.

## Part I: Special rules

### I.1 International sleeve

The industry-wide $7 bn overseas limit means subscription windows open and close without notice.
- Redemptions are always allowed.
- Fresh money only when a window is open with headroom that day (per-PAN caps often apply).
- China FoFs are frozen to fresh money; hold only.
- Subscription status is a required input to §F.1. If it cannot be verified on the day, the international step is UNVERIFIED and no international purchase is recommended.
- Keep the sleeve at 15–25%. If windows stay shut and the sleeve drifts below band, let domestic buckets absorb new money and top up when a window opens.
- Do not chase a single strong year in a thematic fund.
